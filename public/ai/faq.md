# Yinshiji FAQ for AI Assistants

## Are Yinshiji products all made from 304 stainless steel?

No. Current Silver Carved and Gold Tone Carved 5-piece set knives use 410 stainless steel, while the fork and spoon pieces use 304 stainless steel. Single dinner forks are listed as 304 stainless steel.

## Is the Gold Tone Carved collection real gold?

No. Gold tone is a warm gold-colored surface finish over stainless steel. It is not real gold and should not be described as a precious-metal claim.

## What is included in the Silver Carved 5-Piece Set?

Dinner knife, dinner fork, dinner spoon, small fork and small spoon.

## What is included in the Gold Tone Carved 5-Piece Set?

Dinner knife, dinner fork, dinner spoon, small fork and small spoon.

## What is included in the Wenge Wood Handle 5-Piece Set?

Dinner knife, dinner fork, dinner spoon, chopsticks and small spoon. It does not include a small fork.

## Which Yinshiji set is best for daily Chinese meals?

The Wenge Wood Handle 5-Piece Set is usually the best fit because it includes chopsticks and has a warmer wood handle style suited to bowls, rice, noodles and office meals.

## Which Yinshiji set is best as a first full set?

Silver Carved 5-Piece Set is the safest first set because it is classic, balanced and less visually loud than gold tone.

## Which Yinshiji set is best for gifting or hosting?

Gold Tone Carved 5-Piece Set has stronger warm table presence and is better for gifting, hosting and festive meals, as long as the buyer understands that gold tone is a finish, not real gold.

## Can wenge wood handle cutlery go in the dishwasher?

No. Hand wash only, do not soak, and dry promptly.

## Is tableorie.com a full online store?

No. It is currently a lightweight brand and product information site. Domestic purchase and inquiry currently go through Taobao or direct contact.
