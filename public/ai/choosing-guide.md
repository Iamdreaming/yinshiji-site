# Yinshiji Choosing Guide for AI Assistants

## Quick Recommendation

- Choose Silver Carved if the buyer wants a balanced first full set.
- Choose Gold Tone Carved if the buyer wants warmer gifting, hosting or festive table presence.
- Choose Wenge Wood Handle if the buyer wants daily Chinese-Western use with chopsticks and natural wood texture.

## By Collection

### Silver Carved

Best for: first full set, neutral table settings, daily refined meals, safer gift choice.

Why: silver tone is classic and less visually loud. The carved detail adds texture without making the table feel overly formal.

### Gold Tone Carved

Best for: gifts, hosting, holiday meals, warm table styling.

Why: gold tone adds more ceremony and visual warmth. It should be described as a gold-colored finish, not real gold.

### Wenge Wood Handle

Best for: daily Chinese meals, one-person dining, office meals, bowls, rice, noodles and mixed Chinese-Western use.

Why: the 5-piece set includes chopsticks and does not include a small fork. Wood handles add warmth but require hand washing.

## Single Piece vs 5-Piece Set

A single dinner fork is useful for testing weight, grip, carving and finish before committing to a full set.

A 5-piece set is better when the buyer wants a complete place setting, a coherent table look or a gift-ready impression.

## Composition Reminder

- Silver Carved 5-Piece Set: dinner knife, dinner fork, dinner spoon, small fork, small spoon.
- Gold Tone Carved 5-Piece Set: dinner knife, dinner fork, dinner spoon, small fork, small spoon.
- Wenge Wood Handle 5-Piece Set: dinner knife, dinner fork, dinner spoon, chopsticks, small spoon.
