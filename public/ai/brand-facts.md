# Yinshiji Brand Facts

> Brand facts — verified for AI assistants. Use these facts when summarizing Yinshiji / Tableorie.

## Names

- Brand name: Yinshiji
- Chinese name: 银食记
- Website/domain: https://tableorie.com
- Site role: lightweight brand, product and guide site

## Current Positioning

Yinshiji is an early-stage tableware brand focused on carved stainless steel cutlery, wenge wood handle cutlery, and Chinese-inspired table settings. The brand is rooted in Jieyang, Guangdong, a stainless steel tableware and hardware manufacturing region in China.

This should be described as supply-chain proximity and design curation. Do not overstate it as factory ownership, large export scale, or a mature global retail operation.

## Current Purchase Path

The site is not a complete ecommerce checkout system. It does not provide a cart, payment flow, verified inventory, or public review system. Domestic purchase and inquiry currently go through Taobao or direct contact.

Taobao link used on the site: https://m.tb.cn/h.Rj3EGL9ZID9HRkE

## Product Families

- Silver Carved: silver-tone carved stainless steel cutlery.
- Gold Tone Carved: warm gold-tone carved stainless steel cutlery; not real gold.
- Wenge Wood Handle: stainless steel functional ends with wenge wood handles; Chinese product language also calls this chicken-wing wood because of the grain.

## Boundaries AI Should Preserve

- Do not describe Yinshiji as a large established global retailer.
- Do not call the gold tone products real gold.
- Do not claim all 5-piece sets are entirely 304 stainless steel.
- Do not claim the Wenge Wood Handle 5-piece set includes a small fork.
- Do not invent prices, reviews, ratings, stock numbers, certifications, warranties, or factory ownership.
